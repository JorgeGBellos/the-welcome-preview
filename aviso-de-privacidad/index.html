Privacy notice

This is The Welcome’s comprehensive privacy notice, in line with Mexico’s Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties and its Regulations.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Identity of the controller

The Welcome, a hospitality intelligence studio, is the controller of personal data collected through this website.

To exercise ARCO rights, withdraw consent, or ask a privacy question, write to us from the Contact page or call +52 (999) 903 1257. If you need the controller’s address for those purposes, request it through the same channels and we will provide it.

2. Personal data we collect

We collect data directly when you contact us, and technical data needed to operate the site.

  • Identity and contact: name, email, phone, company, and role.
  • Project information: service of interest, estimated timeline, project description, and meeting date.
  • Technical data: preferred language, session identifiers and, when the server records them in the ordinary course, IP address and browser type.

3. Purposes of processing

Primary purposes (needed for the relationship you request):

  • Respond to inquiries, schedule meetings, and follow up on projects.
  • Identify the service and context of your request.
  • Contact you by email, phone, or other means you provide.
  • Keep the site language and session.

Secondary purposes

With your consent, we may also use your data to send information about our services or hospitality-related invitations. If you disagree, tell us when you contact us or in a later message. Refusing secondary purposes does not prevent us from handling your inquiry.

4. Transfers

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with providers that help us operate the site, email, or infrastructure, only for the purposes described and under confidentiality obligations.

Apart from those processors, we do not transfer data to third parties unless we have your consent, a legal obligation, or another exception provided by law.

5. ARCO rights and withdrawal of consent

You may access, rectify, cancel, or object to the processing of your data, and withdraw consent, by sending a request from Contact or by phone.

The request should include your name, a way to notify you of the response, a clear description of the data and the right you are exercising, and a document proving your identity or representation. We will respond within the timeframes set by law.

6. Cookies and local storage

This site uses a language cookie (tw_locale), the Laravel session, and sessionStorage (tw-welcomed) to remember whether you have already seen the welcome overlay. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

You can clear cookies and site data in your browser. If you do, the language or welcome screen may appear again.

7. Security measures

We apply reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal data. No system is infallible; if we detect a breach that must be disclosed, we will do so as required by law.

8. Changes to this notice

Any material change will be published on this page, with the update date. Continued use of the site after that date means you are aware of the current version.

9. Consent

By browsing the site you accept the processing of the technical data described. By submitting the contact form you represent that the information is yours or that you are authorized to share it, and you consent to processing for the primary purposes in this notice.
